Aurora → Sales Breakdown

One-click capture · Volt FL

This bookmarklet reads the Aurora project you are looking at and drops the figures straight into the Sales Breakdown widget: address, utility, consumption, production, offset, and the whole Compare pre and post-solar card. Nothing is typed twice, and nothing is guessed.

⚡ Aurora → Volt
Drag that button to your bookmarks bar.
If the bar is hidden, press ⌘⇧B (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows) first.
Clicking it here does nothing — it only works on an Aurora page.

How to use it

  1. Open the project in Aurora and land on the Dashboard. Click the bookmarklet — it grabs the customer, address, utility, bill and annual consumption.
  2. Click Sales Mode and scroll to Compare pre and post-solar. Click the bookmarklet again — it adds production, offset, escalation, grid use, fixed costs and every financing option.
  3. A small panel in the corner shows what has been captured and what is still missing. Hit Send to Sales Breakdown and the widget opens with everything filled in.
Two clicks, not one, and that is on purpose. Aurora keeps the project data and the money on different screens, so the bookmarklet accumulates: run it wherever you are, as often as you like, and it merges what it finds. The panel tells you when it has enough.

What it captures

ScreenFields
DashboardCustomer · address · utility & tariff · avg. monthly bill · annual kWh · system size · offset · design cost
Sales ModeGrid use and fixed costs, pre and post · avg. monthly payment · monthly savings · escalation · average rate · yearly production · every financing option with its payment, escalator and $/kWh
The AHJ is deliberately not captured. Aurora's jurisdiction is wrong often enough that the Branch Lookup value wins. The bookmarklet never touches that field.

What it will not do

It reads only what is rendered on the screen you are on, in your own logged-in session. It sends nothing to any server, stores nothing outside your browser, touches no credentials, and never writes to Aurora. If a number is not on screen, it is not captured — scroll to it and click again.

It will break, eventually. This reads Aurora's interface, so an Aurora redesign can knock a field out. When that happens the panel shows the field as missing instead of sending a wrong number, and the Sales Breakdown fields stay editable by hand. Expect a repair once or twice a year.

If the widget does not open

Some browsers block the pop-up. The panel then falls back to Copy JSON: click it, open the Sales Breakdown yourself, and press Paste from Aurora in the Aurora section. Same result.

The Sales Breakdown only accepts messages coming from v2.aurorasolar.com.
